# Velmora (fictional client)

Velmora is a fictional brand created for this assessment. Every product, number and person in these files is invented.

## The brand

- London evening-wear label, launched in October 2025.
- 48 pieces: dresses, jumpsuits, tops, skirts, outerwear and two accessories (`products.json`).
- Most pieces are made to order in the London studio, in 14 to 21 days. Accessories ship from stock.
- Prices from £60 to £690; a typical order is about £335.
- Sells in the UK and the EU through a Shopify store. Shipping: UK 3 to 5 days after making, EU 5 to 8 days.
- Returns: 14 days, unworn, with tags. Made-to-order pieces in a non-standard size cannot be returned.
- Sizes UK 6 to 18.
- Gross margin is about 55% of the selling price.

## Where it stands

- 760 Instagram followers, 41 posts. No customer reviews yet.
- Most visitors arrive from Instagram on a phone (`analytics.csv`, six months by channel and device).
- A paid social test ran in August and September.
- The studio can make at most 20 made-to-order pieces a week.
- Black Friday is six weeks away. The founder wants more sales from the website, and wants Velmora to come up when someone asks an AI assistant for "an evening dress for a winter wedding".

## Audience

Women aged 28 to 45 in the UK and Europe, buying for a specific occasion: a winter wedding, a black-tie dinner, New Year's Eve. They decide on fit, fabric and delivery date, and they want to be sure it arrives in time.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, warm. Short sentences.
- Say the fabric, the fit and the delivery date plainly.
- Never: "luxury", "stunning", "must-have", exclamation marks, invented reviews or invented scarcity ("only 2 left").

## Look

- Palette: ink `#1E1B1A`, ivory `#F6F1EA`, stone `#CFC6BA`, accent oxblood `#6B1F2A`.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond for headings, Inter for text (both free on Google Fonts).
- Photography: there are no photos. Use free stock images with credit (Unsplash, Pexels) or plain placeholders. Do not use any real brand's images or logo.
